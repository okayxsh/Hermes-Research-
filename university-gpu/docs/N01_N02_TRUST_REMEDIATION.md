# N01 and N02 trust remediation

## Reproduction and boundaries

Starting from `ed775ecfbd3569c0f31ac0f0680689e19a7fc69a`, disposable synthetic evidence reproduced N01: after adding an otherwise harmless field to a raw trace, updating its inventory and rehashing the authoritative result, `RunStore.completed()` accepted the changed record. This showed internal consistency, not independent authentication. The same baseline's `_validate_engineering_approval` accepted a synthetic self-attested approval with nonempty typed values (N02). Neither reproduction started a GPU or research episode.

Raw logs and mandatory hashes continue to detect missing, truncated and inconsistent traces. The new `rq1.university_gpu.evidence_anchor` command creates create-only per-unit records binding the frozen manifest, run, unit, episode, attempt, exact result-file hash and hashes/sizes of the attempt files. It checks the frozen manifest and evidence before producing a record. A supplied external directory is checked for conflicts on completed-result reads, resume validation and export. The status, validation and partial-export reports distinguish internally validated evidence, matched records and unanchored completed units.

These records are **not independently authenticated merely because they exist or are outside the run directory**. The operator must copy and verify them in independently protected storage, then configure `RQ1_EVIDENCE_ANCHOR_DIR` to the independently trusted copy. A party able to replace both the run and that copy can defeat comparison. Missing per-unit records are explicitly reported as unanchored; a missing configured directory or conflicting available record fails closed. A removed anchor cannot be distinguished from one never created without a separately protected inventory. Keep protected copies and independent inventories; do not overwrite conflicts. Partial exports include available anchor copies for portability, but those copies are not themselves an independent trust source.

## Engineering authorization

Freeze requires a completed engineering-only approval record and `RQ1_TRUSTED_ENGINEERING_APPROVAL_SHA256`, obtained independently from an authorized reviewer. The canonical JSON digest is computed by `rq1.university_gpu.authorization.approval_digest`. The record binds candidate hash, run ID, profile, model tag/digest/quantization, 200-action cap, D0/D18, engineering-only interpretation, approver and approval provenance. The invalid template remains test-only until an authorized human completes and approves it. Test-only records fail production checks.

Benchmark, launch and resume additionally require `RQ1_TRUSTED_ENGINEERING_MANIFEST_SHA256` for the reviewed frozen manifest and explicit `--yes` from the GPU operator. Structural and read-only diagnostics do not require those pins. These are operator-controlled trust pins, not signatures: the code compares exact contents to the configured values but cannot establish who supplied them or whether the channel was authenticated. A person able to alter the pins can authorize a different record. Genuine human approval and independent pin delivery remain operationally pending; no approval was created in this work. Engineering approval does not activate scientific six-arm experiments.

The precise reviewer, operator, anchor-transfer and interrupted-run procedures are in `NEXT_VISIT_RUNBOOK.md`. The system does not claim an independent copy exists until a human has verified it.
